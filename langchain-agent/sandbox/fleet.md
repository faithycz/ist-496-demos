# Tesla Model Y vs. Ford Mustang Mach-E for a 100-Car Sales Fleet

## Quick comparison

| Category | Tesla Model Y | Ford Mustang Mach-E |
|---|---|---|
| Range | About 260–310 miles depending on trim | Up to about 320 miles max EPA-est. depending on trim |
| Cargo space | ~34 cu ft behind seats; up to ~76 cu ft max | 29.7 cu ft behind rear seats; up to 59.7 cu ft max, plus frunk |
| Seating | 5-passenger standard | 5-passenger |
| Charging | Tesla Supercharger access; fast DC charging | BlueOval Charge Network support; DC fast charging |
| Driver assist | Traffic-Aware Cruise Control; available Full Self-Driving (Supervised) | Ford Co-Pilot360 features such as adaptive cruise and lane-centering |
| Fleet/maintenance | Lower routine maintenance; fleet tools/API options; service experience can vary | Lower routine maintenance; generally straightforward for fleet charging and service |

## Fleet fit

**Tesla Model Y**
- Stronger charging-network advantage thanks to the Supercharger network.
- Better cargo capacity for samples, luggage, and sales materials.
- Potentially better for long-distance reps who need fast charging and efficient trip planning.
- Downsides: higher purchase price, service lead times can vary, and the interface may require driver training.

**Ford Mustang Mach-E**
- Competitive range and a familiar Ford ecosystem for fleet management.
- Good standard 5-passenger layout with respectable cargo room.
- Usually easier to onboard drivers who want a more conventional in-cabin experience.
- Downsides: less cargo capacity than the Model Y and charging experience is not as integrated.

## Recommendation

For a **100-car sales fleet**, the **Tesla Model Y is the better overall choice** if your priority is **maximizing charging convenience, cargo flexibility, and long-route usability**. The **Ford Mustang Mach-E** is the better alternative if you want a more traditional dealer-backed service relationship and a slightly more conventional driver experience.

**Recommended pick: Tesla Model Y.**
